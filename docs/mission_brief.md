# TenderScan — Mission Brief

## Project Overview

**TenderScan** is a lightweight tender scraping and aggregation system that helps South African SMMEs, contractors, and service providers discover relevant government opportunities faster — without the manual search hassle.

## Problem Statement

South African tenderpreneurs spend hours manually searching multiple portals (eTenders.gov.za, provincial bulletins, SETA portals) for opportunities that match their business profile. This is inefficient, error-prone, and causes businesses to miss deadlines.

## Mission

Reduce tender discovery time from **hours to minutes** by automating metadata collection, profile-based filtering, and personalised delivery of opportunities.

## Target Users

| Segment | Description |
|---|---|
| SMMEs | Small and medium enterprises seeking government contracts |
| Contractors | Construction, civil, electrical, and related trades |
| ICT Providers | Software, hardware, and digital services companies |
| Consultants | Professional services (legal, financial, HR) |
| NGOs / NPOs | Non-profits applying for government grants |

## Key Metrics (Success Criteria)

- Tender discovery time reduced from ~3 hours to < 10 minutes per session
- Coverage of ≥ 3 major portals in Phase 1
- User profile match accuracy ≥ 80% (relevant results / total results)
- Zero document redistribution (links only to official sources)

## Guiding Principles

1. **Public data only** — scrape metadata, never host or redistribute official documents
2. **Official source attribution** — always credit the National Treasury eTender Portal
3. **Rate limiting** — respect server load with request delays and robots.txt compliance
4. **User privacy** — POPIA-compliant data handling, encryption at rest
5. **Accuracy over volume** — relevant results matter more than sheer quantity
